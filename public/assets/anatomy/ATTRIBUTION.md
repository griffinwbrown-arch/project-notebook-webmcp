# Anatomy atlas attribution

`authority-atlas-206.glb` is derived from the Z-Anatomy human anatomy models.

- Source: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
- Creator: Z-Anatomy contributors
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Project: Notebook exported 208 source-owned skeletal meshes into a browser-ready GLB, assigned stable semantic bone IDs and section metadata, applied the source transforms during export, converted the scene to glTF's Y-up coordinate system, and replaced source materials with six neutral section materials. The application normalizes display scale at runtime. The export represents 206 logical adult bones because the sternum has three source meshes.

The modified atlas remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Project: Notebook's MIT license does not replace the atlas license.
